Why does accuracy matter?

Typing accuracy isn't just about speed - it's about efficiency. Every backspace, every correction, and every mistyped character slows you down.

The most productive typists aren't necessarily the fastest - they're the most accurate.

How to improve

Focus on accuracy first, speed second. Practice touch typing without looking at the keyboard. Use all your fingers and maintain proper posture.

The fewer corrections you make, the faster you'll ultimately become.
